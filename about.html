About

Meet Your Coach

It started with a single decision at thirteen — walk into the gym. Eight years later, that decision has become German de la Cruz's craft and his mission. Today he coaches clients online, helping them reach their goals without spending years learning everything the hard way, the way he had to.

The turning point

In February 2025, German began the transformation that would change everything — losing over 100 pounds and rebuilding his physique from the ground up. It's more than a number. It's living proof of the exact process he now uses to coach his clients.

German's story

German didn't have a coach when he started. For eight years, every plateau, every dead-end approach, and every hard-won breakthrough came down to trial and error. Progress that could have taken months sometimes took years — simply because he was figuring it all out on his own.

That's exactly why he coaches today. Two years ago, he began working with clients for one reason: to hand them the roadmap he never had. He's genuinely driven to help people skip the mistakes that set him back — and he treats every client as a chance to keep learning and sharpening his craft.

How German coaches

German's approach is evidence-based, individualized, and built for the long term. No fads and no cookie-cutter templates — just data-driven decisions tailored to your body, your goals, and your day-to-day life.

What sets him apart is that he genuinely cares about where you're trying to go. He'll give you the tools, the structure, and the honest accountability to get there, and he'll always be straight with you about what it takes. The goals are yours — his job is to make sure you have everything you need to reach them.

German's credential isn't a certificate on a wall. It's eight years of real, in-the-trenches experience and a transformation he's lived firsthand.

Who German works with

German coaches entirely online, so his guidance travels with you wherever you are. He's an especially strong fit for two kinds of people:

  • Those who feel like they've tried everything. If you've exhausted your options and still aren't seeing results, German will help you find what's actually been missing.
  • Experienced lifters who want refinement. Already making progress? He'll fine-tune your plan so you keep moving forward instead of stalling out.

What German specializes in

Fat loss transformations

German's signature — and the area he leads with. Having lost over 100 pounds himself, he knows sustainable fat loss inside and out: steady, livable progress without crash diets or extreme restriction. He also guides clients through reverse dieting and metabolic recovery once the weight is off, so the results actually last instead of bouncing back.

Strength training

German helps lifters get stronger by fixing what quietly holds them back. He refines technique and lifting mechanics first, then diagnoses and breaks through the plateaus that stall progress — so your numbers keep climbing safely.

Physique & muscle building

For clients chasing a more developed look, German runs structured lean-gaining phases that build quality muscle with intention, rather than just piling on size.

Beyond the gym

Great training is only part of the picture. His coaching also covers:

  • Nutrition & macros — sustainable eating and targets built around your life.
  • Bloodwork & health markers — decisions informed by what's actually happening inside.
  • Gut health & digestion — troubleshooting the issues that quietly derail progress.
  • Habits, lifestyle & mindset — the sleep, consistency, and accountability that hold it all together.

Enhanced athlete coaching

For experienced and enhanced athletes, German offers responsible, health-first guidance — anchored by regular bloodwork and health markers, with your long-term wellbeing as the priority. Specific protocols are discussed privately during a consultation.
